# Focus & Snuggery
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, Dexie, vite-plugin-pwa, optional WebLLM.
## Users
People with ADHD, autism, and cognitive or sensory disabilities managing everyday tasks.
## Product Purpose
Turn messy thoughts into calm, doable action, showing one task at a time.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Brain dump with starter prompts, Enter-to-review/Shift+Enter-newline, editable steps, unclear-fragment clarification and Save-to-Next, one-task focus with optional rhythms, thought capture to Later, energy ordering, check-in, calming tools, adaptive settings and a skippable native welcome dialog. Welcome offers an optional first name (48 characters maximum, stored locally), explicit natural-voice setup and a direct route to the first brain dump. Escape and Skip setup dismiss it. Previously onboarded users retain access without forced setup; Settings can edit the name and replay Show introduction. Read-aloud covers tasks, reviewed steps and grounding prompts, with local device speech or explicitly downloaded Kokoro natural speech, selectable British/American styles and pace, visible preparation, Stop and removal controls. Flexible task timing remains the default; optional Gentle 10/3 and Pomodoro 25/5 rhythms require the user to start every phase, with a 15-minute Pomodoro rest after four focus rounds. Local-first, no accounts, tracking, paid APIs or medical claims. Optional WebLLM and Kokoro downloads disclose initial network, storage and device requirements; dictation uses explicitly loaded local Whisper tiny.en (about 40 MB plus runtime), up to 30 seconds of English recording, and editable transcript review before adding text. Initial resource downloads contact model/runtime hosts; the app does not upload recordings. Loading keeps the microphone off.
## Brand Commitments
Focus & Snuggery working name. Calm, plain, respectful copy. No guilt, pressure or autoplay motion.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA target, 48px targets, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, pause/extend timer and adaptive sensory controls. Real-user testing is required before claiming suitability.
## Open Decisions
Release destination: GitHub Pages at https://sayamdev.github.io/focus-and-snuggery/. Individual task duration estimates are suggestions, not guarantees. Focus rhythms are optional structures, not medical guidance. Session rounds reset on reload. Natural speech requires explicit initialization after reload; browser cache eviction may require another download. Human listening quality, low-end/mobile performance and participant suitability remain to be validated.
